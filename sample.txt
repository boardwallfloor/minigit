Title: The Last Voyage

The clock struck midnight as the last ferry pulled into the quiet dock. The boat was nearly empty—just a man in a worn-out coat and a girl clutching a red backpack. They didn’t look at each other, yet both seemed to carry the same weight of tired decisions.

As the boat groaned back to life and pushed off into the dark water, the man glanced at the girl. “Missed your stop?” he asked.

She shook her head. “Didn’t get on for the destination. Just needed to move.”

He nodded. “Same.”

Outside, the harbor lights blurred into streaks of gold and blue. Neither spoke again, but when the boat docked at the final pier, they stood up in unison—strangers with no map, walking into the unknown.
